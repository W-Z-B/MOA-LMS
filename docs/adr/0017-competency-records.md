# ADR 0017: Competency records beside marks

**Status:** proposed (decision D8); not yet taken. Items 0.13 and 3.13 wait for it.
**Date:** 5 October 2026.

## Context

Practical skills in the Caribbean are assessed against occupational standards under the Council for TVET's
competency model: each unit competent or not yet competent, with evidence and the assessor. It is the basis
of NVQ and CVQ awards. Which GSA programmes follow it is a question for GSA (decision D9).

## Recommended answer

Keep competency records beside marks for the programmes that follow the model: each unit recorded as
competent or not yet competent, with the evidence (observation checklist, logbook entry, photograph) and
the assessor, marked in the field on a phone and offline (Release 2, practical assessment first).

## Consequences if taken

- A programme can be marked in percentages, in competencies, or both.
- What is sent to the SRMS for a competency-based unit is agreed with the Registrar.
