# GSA LMS quick guide: Course administrator

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: course administrators. One page.

You sign in with your password and the **Authenticator code** from your phone. If you look after more
than one campus, choose **All campuses** or one campus at the top of the page (on a phone, in the account
menu).

**1. Start the day.** Home shows **Course sites**, **Students**, **Without a lecturer** and **Takedown
requests**. **To do** lists what waits, including help requests.

**2. Answer help requests.** Open them from **To do**, or search "Help requests". Each shows the question and the page it came from.
Your answer reaches the person as a notification.

**3. Record an accommodation.** Search "Accommodations", **Add an accommodation**: **More time in quizzes
(%)**, **More days for each assignment** or **Another format needed**. The **Reason** is never shown to
lecturers. Tick **In force**, **Save**.

**4. Handle a takedown.** **Admin**, **Takedown requests**: **Restore the item** or **Withdraw the item**.

**5. Change a course's storage.** **Admin**, **Storage allowances**: **Find a course**, **Allowance in
MB**, **Save allowance**.

**6. Keep the course templates.** **Admin**, **Course templates**. Lecturers apply them in **Course setup**.

**7. Require training.** Staff development: **Required training**, **Require a course**, **Assign now**.

**8. Sign off the access review.** **Admin**, **Access review**; check who holds which role; **Sign off
the review**.

**9. Deal with a correction request.** **Admin**, **Correction requests**: **Corrected** or **Not changed**.

**10. Check the links with the SRMS and HRMS.** **Admin**, **Integration runs**: did the last runs work?

## Where to find things

| Looking for | Go to |
|---|---|
| Help requests, Accommodations, the GSA rubric library | Search (Ctrl K) |
| Templates, takedowns, storage, access review, corrections | **Admin** |
| Certificate templates, certificates issued (**Withdraw**) | Staff development |
| A competency framework | **Import a framework** (CSV) |
| Old material in folders | The folder import is run on the server (import_folder), one folder per module. Files arrive as drafts with licence "Not yet known" for the lecturer to check |

## Training and champions (item 7.15)

You run the three training sessions by department, on the training system with fictional data only, and
the weekly clinic. You are the champions' direct line: answer their questions first. Collect the ticked
course-site checklists at the end of week 2 and report the numbers to the heads of department.

## Without signal

Answering help requests and the **Admin** pages need signal. Messages and your own **Ask for help**
questions wait on the phone and send when the signal returns.

## Help

**Help** at the top of every page has a page for course administrators. For problems with the system
itself, contact the LMS administrators.
