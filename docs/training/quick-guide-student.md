# GSA LMS quick guide: Student

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: every GSA student. One page; keep it.

Your courses appear once the Registry enrols you. At your first sign-in you are put on the course
**Getting started with the GSA LMS**: do it first. It has a practice quiz and a practice logbook entry.

**1. Sign in.** Type your **Username** and **Password**, press **Sign in**. Forgotten it? **Forgot your
password?**, then **Email me a link**. No email address on file? Ask the course administrator.

**2. See what is due.** Open **To do** (top of the page, or the bottom bar on a phone). Oldest first.

**3. Read the course.** Open the course, **Content** tab. Open each item; press **Mark complete** when done.

**4. Hand in work.** **Assignments** tab, **Open**. Type your answer or attach files, tick the statement,
press **Hand in**. Keep the receipt: **Print the receipt**, or find it later in **Every hand-in and its
receipt**.

**5. Read your feedback.** Open the assignment again. You see the mark, the feedback and any spoken
feedback. The **Gradebook** tab shows your marks and **How your coursework total is worked out**.

**6. Take a quiz.** **Quizzes** tab, **Start the quiz**. Use **Next page**; at the end **Finish
attempt...**, then **Submit my answers**. Watch **Time left**.

**7. Check in to a class.** **Classes** tab, **Check in**, type the **Code on the screen**, **Check in**.
It opens 15 minutes before the class and closes at the end.

**8. Keep your logbook.** **Logbook** tab, **Add an entry**. Fill in the date, hours, where and what you
did; **Take a photo** if useful; **Save entry**.

**9. Ask your lecturer.** **Messages**, **New message**, choose the course, write, **Send**. For more time
on an assignment, ask your lecturer this way.

**10. Ask a question in class.** **Discussion** tab, open the forum, **Reply** or **Start a discussion**.

## Where to find things

| Looking for | Go to |
|---|---|
| Anything (a course, assignment, page) | Search: the bar at the top, Ctrl K, or **Search** on a phone |
| Dates and classes | Search for "Calendar" |
| Your password, devices, email | **Me** (phone) or your name at the top, then My account |
| What the LMS holds about you | **My data** on Home; **Download my data** |

## Without signal

- **Works, and sends later:** typed answers, quiz answers once started, logbook entries, messages and
  **Ask for help**. The top of the page shows **N waiting to send**. Do not sign out until it is 0.
- **Logbook photos** wait on the phone and send after the entry.
- **Needs signal:** attaching files, starting a quiz, checking in to a class, forum posts.
- Hand in early if your signal is weak. The deadline is the deadline.

## Help

- **Help** at the top of every page explains each screen.
- **Ask for help** sends your question to the course administrators, with the page you were on.
- Your lecturer, by **Messages**, for anything about the course itself.
