# GSA LMS quick guide: Administrator

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: the LMS administrators. One page.

You can do everything a course administrator can (see that guide), and the tasks below. You sign in with
your password and the **Authenticator code** from your phone. Never share either, and never sign in on a
shared computer without signing out after.

**1. Invite new people.** **Admin**, **People to invite**: choose the **Campus** and the **Term code**,
**See who would be invited**, check the list, then **Send N invitations**. Each person gets a link to
choose a password.

**2. Help someone who cannot sign in.** They can use **Forgot your password?** themselves. If they have no
email address on file, or have lost the phone with their authenticator, deal with it in person, after
checking who they are.

**3. Check the audit log.** **Admin**, **Audit log**: use the filters to narrow it; **Check the chain
now** shows whether the log is whole; **Export to a spreadsheet (CSV)** for a review.

**4. Publish a new privacy notice.** **Admin**, **Privacy notice**: **Write a new version**, then
**Publish**. Everyone reads it again at their next sign-in. Agree the text with the DPO first.

**5. Run retention and disposal.** **Admin**, **Retention and disposal**: **Find what is due**, then
**Keep** what must stay. A different person must **Approve the disposal**. **Cancel the run** stops it.

**6. Record a data breach.** **Admin**, **Breach register**: **Record a breach** the same day. Then, as
each is done: **Contained now**, **Commissioner told now**, **People told now**, and **Close**.

**7. Sign off the access review.** **Admin**, **Access review**: check each role holder; **Sign off the
review**.

**8. Check the links with the SRMS and HRMS.** **Admin**, **Integration runs**.

## Where to find things

| Looking for | Go to |
|---|---|
| Invitations, audit log, privacy notice, retention, breaches | **Admin** |
| Help requests, Accommodations, rubric library | Search (Ctrl K) |
| One campus or all | **All campuses** or a campus button at the top (on a phone, the account menu) |
| Your own devices and sign-ins | My account: **Signed-in devices**, **Sign out everywhere else** |

## Training (item 7.15)

Training uses the training (staging) system and fictional data only. Make sure no real record is ever
loaded there, and that each trainee's training account is ready before their department's session.

## Without signal

The **Admin** pages need signal. Do administration at a desk with good signal or a wired network.

## Help

**Help** at the top of every page has a page for administrators. The course administrators collect
problems from lecturers and champions and pass on those that need you.
