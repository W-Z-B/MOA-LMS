# GSA LMS quick guide: Head of department

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: heads of department. One page.

**There is no separate "head of department" role or screen in the LMS.** You use it in three ways:

- **As a lecturer**, for the courses you teach. The lecturer quick guide covers this.
- **As a supervisor**, approving your staff's requests to join staff-development courses, and naming a
  stand-in when you are away.
- **As the keeper of a department question bank**, shared with your teaching staff.

You sign in as a lecturer does: password, then the **Authenticator code** from your phone.

**1. See what waits for you.** **To do** lists your teaching work, oldest first. Requests from your staff
to join courses are in **Approvals** (step 2): look there each week.

**2. Approve a request to join a course.** Search for "Staff development" (Ctrl K), then the **Approvals**
tab. Under **Waiting for your decision**: **Approve**, or write a **Comment (needed to turn it down)**
and **Turn down**.

**3. Name a stand-in before leave or travel.** **Approvals** tab: **Name a stand-in**, **Find** the person,
**Choose**, set **From** and **To**, **Why (leave, travel)**, **Name the stand-in**. **End** stops it early.

**4. Make a department question bank.** In one of your courses, **Quizzes** tab, **Question banks**,
**New bank**. Choose "A department, shared with its teaching staff" and type the **Department code**.

**5. Add questions to it.** Open the bank, **New question**, or **Import** a file (Moodle XML, GIFT or
QTI 2.1, up to 5 MB). **Export** to keep a copy.

**6. Follow the course-site standard.** Ask your champion for the department's ticked checklists at the
end of week 2 of term. Every pilot course should meet the standard by then.

**7. Look at your own courses' activity.** On Home, **Not seen in N days** shows students not logging in.

**8. Join a course yourself.** **Staff development**, **Catalogue**, **Join the course** or **Ask to join**.

## Your part in the training (item 7.15)

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Name a department champion and tell the course administrators | Before the first session |
| Free your lecturers for the three training sessions | Before term |
| Allow the champion about 2 hours a week in the pilot term | Pilot term |
| Read the training summary, and the week 2 results | After session 3; end of week 2 |

## Where to find things

| Looking for | Go to |
|---|---|
| Staff development and **Approvals** | Search: "Staff development" |
| Your department's question banks | Any of your courses: **Quizzes**, **Question banks** |
| Your courses | **My courses** |

## Without signal

Approvals and question banks need signal. Messages and **Ask for help** wait on the phone and send when
the signal returns. For teaching, see the lecturer guide.

## Help

**Help** at the top of every page has a page for heads of department. **Ask for help** sends your
question to the course administrators. Your department champion is the first person to ask.
