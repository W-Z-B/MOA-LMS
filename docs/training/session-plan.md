# Lecturer training: three sessions by department

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: the course administrators who run the
training, the department champions who help, and heads of department who arrange time for their staff.

This is the training for checklist item **7.15**: lecturer training in small groups by department. It is
three sessions of about two hours, then a weekly clinic. At the end, each lecturer can build a course site
to the [course-site standard](course-site-standard.md), mark and return work, and run a teaching week,
including where there is no signal.

## How the sessions are run

- **By department, in small groups.** Six to ten lecturers from one department, with their champion.
  People who teach the same kind of course learn together, and the examples fit their work.
- **On the training system, with fictional data only.** All exercises use the demonstration courses and
  people on the training (staging) system. **No real student record is used in training.** The training
  system has a web address of its own; the trainer writes it on the board, and everyone checks it in the
  address bar before signing in.
- **Hands on.** Each lecturer works on their own device. The trainer shows a task once, then everyone
  does it. Talk is kept short.
- **The trainer** is a course administrator. The department's champion helps people who fall behind.
- **Timing.** Sessions 1 and 2 before term starts; session 3 in the week before term or in week 1. The
  three sessions can be a week apart, or on three mornings of one week.
- **Room.** A computer lab or a room with good Wi-Fi, a projector, and power points for phones.

## Session 1: Getting in and building a course site (about 2 hours)

**By the end, each lecturer can:** sign in with a password and an authenticator code; find their way
around; copy a course; add modules, pages and files with their licence; post an announcement; and check
the site as a student would.

| Minutes | What | Exercise |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Welcome; why the LMS; the course-site standard and the week 2 target | Hand out the lecturer quick guide and the standard |
| 20 | Signing in | Add the training account to the authenticator app on your phone. Sign in: **Sign in**, then **Authenticator code** and **Verify**. Read the privacy notice and press **I have read this notice** |
| 15 | Finding your way | Home, **To do**, My courses, search (Ctrl K), the bell, **Messages**. Find "Introduction to Crop Production" through search. Open **Help** at the top of the page |
| 20 | Copying last year's course | Open this year's empty "Soils and Plant Nutrition". **Course setup**, **Copy from an earlier course**, choose the 2025-26 offering, **Move its dates**, **Copy the course**. Check the new dates |
| 10 | Break | |
| 25 | Modules and content | In AGR101: **New module** "Week 3", set **When students see it**. **Add a page** with a heading, a list and a picture with alternative text. Fix anything the **Accessibility check** says **Must be fixed**. **Save and publish**. **Upload a file** and fill in **Whose material is this?** |
| 10 | An announcement | Post a welcome: **Title**, **Message**, **Post and notify students** |
| 10 | Seeing it as a student does | **See it as students do**; then sign in on your phone as the student Kezia Persaud and find the new page |

**Bring:** a smartphone with an authenticator app installed (the trainer names one beforehand), charged;
a laptop if you have one; your own course outline for this term, on paper or a memory stick.

**The trainer checks at the end:** everyone signed in with an authenticator code; everyone copied AGR102
with moved dates; everyone has a published page in AGR101 with no accessibility finding left, a file with
its licence stated, and an announcement; everyone saw their page on a phone as Kezia.

## Session 2: Assessment, marking and the gradebook (about 2 hours)

**By the end, each lecturer can:** set an assignment with a due date and weight; set up gradebook
categories; build a short quiz from a bank; mark with a rubric, give feedback and release it; take marks
from a spreadsheet; and grant an extension.

| Minutes | What | Exercise |
|---|---|---|

| 20 | An assignment | **New assignment**: **Title**, **Opens**, **Due**, **Maximum mark**, **Weight**, **Gradebook category**, **Instructions**; files allowed; **Late work**; **Create assignment** |
| 10 | The student side, timed | In pairs, one plays Kezia on a phone: open the new assignment, attach a photo, tick the statement, **Hand in**, see the receipt. **Target: under two minutes** |

notes anyone over time, for help at the clinic.

## After the sessions: the clinic

- **When:** one hour a week, same time and place, from the week before term to week 6. Then monthly.
- **Who runs it:** a course administrator, with one champion on a rota.
- **Who comes:** anyone, from any department, with any question. No booking.
- **What happens:** people bring their real course site and work on it with help. Common problems are
  noted and sent to the course administrators, who add them to the help pages.
- **Before week 2:** the clinic is where lecturers finish anything left on their course-site checklist.

## Records

The trainer keeps a list of who attended each session and who passed each end-of-session check, by
department. The course administrators send a short summary to each head of department after session 3,
and at the end of week 2 the number of course sites that meet the standard.
