# GSA LMS quick guide: Lecturer

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: lecturers, instructors and teaching
assistants. One page; keep it by your desk. Your course site must meet the course-site standard by week 2.

**1. Sign in.** **Username**, **Password**, **Sign in**; then the 6-digit **Authenticator code** from the
app on your phone, and **Verify**. Lost the phone? Tell the course administrators at once.

**2. Set up the course.** On the site, open **Course setup**. **Copy from an earlier course** (tick **Move
its dates**), or **Start from a template**. Then **Publish to students**.

**3. Add content.** **Content** tab: **New module** and **When students see it**. Then **Add a page**,
**Upload a file** or **Add a link**. For a file, answer **Whose material is this?**. Use **Save and
publish**, or **Publish** on the item.

**4. Set an assignment.** **Assignments** tab, **New assignment**: **Opens**, **Due**, **Maximum mark**,
**Weight**, **Gradebook category**, **Instructions**. **Create assignment**.

**5. Mark and return work.** On the assignment, **Mark**. Give the mark (or the rubric levels) and
**Feedback**, then **Save and release**. **Next not marked** takes you on. For the whole class: **Release
all marks**, or marks from a **Spreadsheet (CSV)**.

**6. Give an extension.** **Extensions**: **For**, **New due date**, **Reason**, **Grant the extension**.

**7. Tell the class.** **Announcements** tab: **Title**, **Message**, **Post and notify students**.

**8. Take the register.** **Classes** tab, open the class, **Show the check-in code in the room**.
Afterwards **Mark the rest present**, correct any, **Save register**.

**9. Mark a practical in the field.** **Practicals** tab, open the task, **Mark in the field**. **Who are
you observing?**, **Met** or **Not met**, **Save observation**, **Next student**.

**10. Sign off logbooks.** **Logbook** tab, **Waiting for sign-off**: **Sign off** or **Return with a
comment**.

## Where to find things

| Looking for | Go to |
|---|---|
| Work waiting to be marked | **To do**, or **Waiting to be marked** on Home |
| Students not logging in | **Not seen in N days** on Home |
| Weights and totals | **Gradebook** tab: **Categories and weights**, **Export to a spreadsheet** |
| Quizzes and question banks | **Quizzes** tab; **Question banks** |
| Calendar, Discussion, rubric library | Search for them (Ctrl K) |
| Sending coursework marks to the SRMS | **Gradebook** tab: **Send coursework to the SRMS** (lecturers only; it locks the marks) |

## Without signal

- **Works, and sends later:** the register (open it once with signal first), practical observations
  (open the task once with signal: **Open it once with signal to mark it in the field.**), messages,
  and **Ask for help**. The header shows **N waiting to send**.
- **Photos** for observations wait on the phone and send after the record.
- **Needs signal:** closing a register, forum posts, uploading files. Mark at a desk with signal.
- Do not sign out with work waiting to send. Signing out also clears field copies from the phone.

## Help

- **Help** at the top of every page; **Ask for help** sends your question and the page to the course
  administrators.
- Your department champion, first. The weekly clinic, for anything bigger.
