# GSA LMS: training pack

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: the course administrators who run the
training, the department champions, heads of department, and anyone at GSA who teaches or supports
teaching with the LMS.

This pack is how GSA brings lecturers, students and staff onto the LMS. It answers checklist item
**7.15**: lecturer training in small groups by department, a GSA course-site standard, and a champion in
each department. It is written so that GSA can run the training itself, without outside help.

## How to use it

1. **Before term.** Each head of department names a champion (see the champion role). The course
   administrators book the three sessions for each department.
2. **Training.** Lecturers attend the three sessions with their own department, on the training system,
   using only the fictional demonstration data. No real student record is used in training.
3. **Building the course sites.** Each lecturer builds their real course site to the course-site
   standard. The champion checks it with them and ticks the list.
4. **Week 2 of term.** Every course in the pilot meets the standard. This is the quality bar "Used" in
   the plan: outline, weekly content and assessment in the LMS by week two, and students logging in
   weekly.
5. **After that.** A weekly clinic for anyone stuck, and the champion as the first person to ask.

## The quick guides

Each quick guide fits on one side of A4. Print them in black and white; they have no pictures. Give the
student guide out at orientation, and the lecturer guide in session 1. The guides list only what the
screens do today. Labels on buttons are shown in **bold**, exactly as they appear on the screen.

## Help inside the LMS

The LMS now has its own help:

- **Help** is a link at the top of every page. It opens a help page for your role, and help pages also
  come up in search.
- **Ask for help** sends your question, with the page you were on, to the course administrators. On a
  phone without signal it waits and is sent when the signal returns. The answer comes back as a
  notification.
- Every new student is put on the course **Getting started with the GSA LMS** at their first sign-in.

## Two targets to keep in mind

From the plan's quality bar "Usable by everyone":

- A student hands in work unaided in **under two minutes**.
- A lecturer marks and returns a submission in **under three minutes**.

Session 2 times both, with the demonstration data.

## Keeping the pack up to date

The course administrators own this pack. When a screen changes, they update the quick guide that
mentions it and change the version and date on its first lines. Champions send them anything that is
wrong or unclear.
