# GSA LMS quick guide: Auditor and data protection officer

**Version 1.0, 6 October 2026, draft for GSA's review.** For: the auditor and the data protection officer
(DPO). One page.

You sign in with your password and the **Authenticator code** from your phone. Reach **Admin** through
search (Ctrl K, type "Admin"). If there is more than one campus, choose **All campuses** or one campus at
the top of the page.

**The auditor** can read, but not change: integration runs, the audit log, the access review, the privacy
notice, correction requests, retention, the breach register and the staff-development records. The
auditor cannot write messages.

**The DPO** writes and publishes the privacy notice, runs retention and disposal, and keeps the breach
register. The DPO can read correction requests but does not decide them. **To do** shows corrections and
disposals waiting.

## Common tasks

**1. (Both) Check the audit log.** **Admin**, **Audit log**. Use the filters; **Check the chain now**
shows whether the log is whole; **Export to a spreadsheet (CSV)**.

**2. (Both) Read the access review.** **Admin**, **Access review**. Course administrators sign it off.

**3. (Both) Check the integration runs.** **Admin**, **Integration runs**.

**4. (Both) See correction requests.** **Admin**, **Correction requests**: what was asked and what was done.

**5. (DPO) Publish a new privacy notice.** **Admin**, **Privacy notice**: **Write a new version**,
**Publish**. Everyone reads it at their next sign-in.

**6. (DPO) Run retention and disposal.** **Admin**, **Retention and disposal**. **Keep** what must stay;
**Approve the disposal** (it must be a different person from the one who started the run), or **Cancel
the run**.

**7. (DPO) Record a breach.** **Admin**, **Breach register**: **Record a breach**. Then, as each is done:
**Contained now**, **Commissioner told now**, **People told now**, **Close**.

**8. (Both) See what the LMS holds about you.** **My data**: **Download my data**.

## Training (item 7.15)

Training uses the training (staging) system and fictional data only. The DPO may ask to see the training
system at any time to confirm that no real student record is there.

## Without signal

The **Admin** pages need signal. Use them at a desk with good signal or a wired network.

## Help

**Help** at the top of every page has a page for the auditor and the DPO. **Ask for help** sends your
question, with the page you were on, to the course administrators.
